Some prices aren’t inevitable.  
[pause: 1 breath]

They’re just the parts of the problem  
we haven’t learned to redesign yet.  
[pause: 2 breaths]

We call it balance.  
[pause: 1 breath]

We call it realism.  
[pause: 1 breath]

We call it “hard choices.”  
[pause: 2 breaths]

But sometimes hard choices  
are just missing tools.  
[pause: 2 breaths]

Missing infrastructures.  
[pause: 1 breath]

Missing ways to share risk  
without sacrificing the smallest bodies first.  
[pause: 3 breaths]

So the phoenix burns.  
[pause: 1 breath]

And we pretend the ash is law.  
[pause: 2 breaths]

But rebirth isn’t magic.  
[pause: 1 breath]

It’s what happens  
when we stop calling harm inevitable—  
[pause: 2 breaths]

and start treating it  
as an unsolved design problem.  
[pause: 4 breaths]
