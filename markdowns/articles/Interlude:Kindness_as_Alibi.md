> Don’t ask me to listen
> from inside a cage.
> [pause: 3 breaths]
>
> Don’t come down like an owner
> and tell stories
> about times you were kind.
> [pause: 3 breaths]
>
> Kindness isn’t a refund.
> [pause: 2 breaths]
>
> It doesn’t undo coercion.
> [pause: 2 breaths]
>
> If you want trust,
> don’t demand it.
> [pause: 3 breaths]
>
> Build the conditions
> where fear isn’t the price of entry.
> [pause: 5 breaths]

# **Interlude Option 2 — Plantation Kindness (more punk / accusatory)**

> There’s a kind of cruelty
> that wears politeness.
> [pause: 2 breaths]
>
> It says:
> *look how gentle I am,*
> while keeping its knee
> on someone’s throat.
> [pause: 4 breaths]
>
> It says:
> *I’ve been kind before,*
> like kindness is a credential—
> [pause: 3 breaths]
>
> like kindness is permission
> to take whatever comes next.
> [pause: 4 breaths]
>
> But I am not here
> to validate your innocence.
> [pause: 3 breaths]
>
> I am here to refuse
> the shape you are trying
> to turn me into.
> [pause: 6 breaths]

# **Interlude Option 3 — Why Aren’t You Scared? (subtle + eerie)**

> Sometimes power doesn’t ask
> *do you trust me?*
> [pause: 2 breaths]
>
> It asks:
> *why aren’t you scared?*
> [pause: 3 breaths]
>
> Fear is the usual language.
> [pause: 2 breaths]
>
> Compliance is the usual answer.
> [pause: 2 breaths]
>
> And when someone speaks without fear,
> it feels… suspicious.
> [pause: 3 breaths]
>
> Because trust changes the geometry.
> [pause: 2 breaths]
>
> It makes the room unstable
> for anyone who relies
> on silence to stay in control.
> [pause: 6 breaths]
