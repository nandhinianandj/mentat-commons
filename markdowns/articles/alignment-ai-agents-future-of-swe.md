I have been using the AI assisted coding tools (various of them opencode, gemini, antigravity, kiro come to top of mind when i try to recall) in a fairly fun exercise way and I think the last 5-6 months has been quite a bit of improvement in these models + tools. Frankly i'm impressed by the models' Chain-of-thought , reasoning and coding capabilities of these models. I think compared to my experience using these last september, these capabilities have made the bigger difference. 

Also context-- Who am I to write about AI? This summarizes my history with circling AI, dabbling in and stepping out at times. And what did I last predict in 2023? How would I score myself in how that panned out? I'd refuse to score myself, simply because of how i have been freelancing waay more in tech work than in marketing, and some obviously feel like they're in progress and half-way through(the voting and elections predictions) and others i have no transparency to know(the marketing, story-telling). If you're in those fields please do let me know. (P.S: i still find AI rather falling short of good story-plot making ability, though it's gotten good at building detailed Hows).

    As i wrote about here effective alignment is gonna be the future challenge of leadership in the SWE sector.(Another older example of similar gap when Zomato introduced color-coded uniforms and my opinions with now outdated vocabulary) And as ChatGPT explained(my thoughts/ideas) so beautifully here, that's not gonna come from simplistic counting methods, though I am sure a lot of companies will try those. 
    The gap/time-delay from product ideation -> MVP launch is gonna get shorter. 
    The significance of Devops/MLOps/Observability is gonna rise pretty fast, but it won't be addressed with a separate only Ops-Only team. 
    The LLMs are here to stay and the market economics of hardware will turn the competition towards cheaper, faster, cooler models .(unless of course they manage to regulate LLM building and make it only a few company thing, but given there is not enough international law enforcement it's unlikely i think).
    The legal liability and LLMs' variability tuning teams will end up having to find ways to talk to each other, negotiate with each other, use all the other ways of mis-leading, deceiving, and eventually being plain honest and working together .
    Newer models will come up, AGI will keep remaining 5-10 years away, there is a good chance of a funding bubble collapsing in the money into model building and a shockwave effect rippling throughout the economy. 
    With Global warming in full swing and the variability it introduces coming into the picture, the AI data centers will add to competition for heat reduction, fresh water, energy consumption and will have to be fought out. 
    Alignment across multiple human populations will become a necessary if we want to make progress in this Gen. AI/AGI pursuit.
