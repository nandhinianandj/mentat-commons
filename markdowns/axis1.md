/axis-i — Systems & Decision Design
Helping organizations make better decisions with data they can trust

Most organizations don’t struggle because they lack data or tools.
They struggle because decisions are unclear, information arrives too late, or systems optimize the wrong behavior.

Axis I focuses on designing decision systems — the combination of data, models, metrics, and organizational processes that determine how choices are made in practice.

What This Covers

Axis I work typically includes:

data and AI/ML system design,

decision diagnostics and redesign,

metrics, incentives, and feedback loops,

research and frameworks for complex environments.

The emphasis is not on tools alone, but on how decisions move through the organization.

How We Work

Start with diagnosis, not solutions.

Every engagement begins by identifying:

which decisions matter most,

how they are currently made,

where information, authority, or incentives break down,

and what prevents learning from outcomes.

Only then do we design or implement systems.

Core Engagements
Data, AI & ML Systems

Build systems teams can understand, maintain, and act on.

End-to-end data pipelines tied to decision workflows

ML models with clear assumptions, limits, and monitoring

Integration into real processes, not unused dashboards

Outcome: faster decision cycles, fewer black boxes, higher adoption.

Decision Systems & Organizational Design

Make decision-making explicit and improvable.

Map how key decisions are actually made

Identify bottlenecks in information, ownership, or incentives

Redesign decision rights, metrics, and feedback loops

Outcome: clearer ownership, fewer stalled discussions, metrics that reflect real priorities.

Research & Thought Leadership

Support long-term thinking under uncertainty.

Focus areas include:

decision quality in complex systems,

collaborative and inclusive decision-making,

consensus building and negotiated trade-offs,

learning systems inside institutions.

Outcome: frameworks that inform strategy, policy, and leadership development.

Engagement Models

Diagnostic (2–8 weeks)
Clarify decision bottlenecks and recommend focused interventions.

Delivery & Architecture (3–12 months)
Design and implement systems teams can own and evolve.

Embedded Advisory
Ongoing support during scale, transition, or restructuring.

Who This Is For

Startups and scale-ups designing core systems

NGOs and public-interest organizations balancing impact and accountability

Policy-adjacent and research teams working with uncertainty

How to Start

We begin with one question:

Which decision, if improved, would make the biggest difference right now?
